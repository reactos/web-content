The ReactOS Project maintains several mailing lists to coordinate development and send out announcements.

The current active lists are:

* **Announcement List** - [Subscribe](mailto:ros-announce-subscribe@reactos.org) | [Unsubscribe](mailto:ros-announce-unsubscribe@reactos.org) | [Information](https://lists.reactos.org/postorius/lists/ros-announce.reactos.org/) | [Archives](https://lists.reactos.org/hyperkitty/list/ros-announce@reactos.org/)<br>
  Official announcements

* **General List** - [Subscribe](mailto:ros-general-subscribe@reactos.org) | [Unsubscribe](mailto:ros-general-unsubscribe@reactos.org) | [Information](https://lists.reactos.org/postorius/lists/ros-general.reactos.org/) | [Archives](https://lists.reactos.org/hyperkitty/list/ros-general@reactos.org/)<br>
  General discussion about ReactOS

* **Developers List** - [Subscribe](mailto:ros-dev-subscribe@reactos.org) | [Unsubscribe](mailto:ros-dev-unsubscribe@reactos.org) | [Information](https://lists.reactos.org/postorius/lists/ros-dev.reactos.org/) | [Archives](https://lists.reactos.org/hyperkitty/list/ros-dev@reactos.org/)<br>
  Discussions regarding ReactOS development

A list of all current and former mailing lists can be found [here](https://lists.reactos.org/hyperkitty/).

Note that you can only email a list once you are successfully subscribed.
Some spam filters also block confirmation emails; please check your spam/bulk folders when (un)subscribing if a confirmation email is not received.
